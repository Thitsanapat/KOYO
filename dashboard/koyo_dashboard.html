EPS block — temperatures (CONFIRMED)

4 fields at offsets 80-87, confirmed 2026-07-30 by matching 8 real frames' rtc_time_unix exactly (0-second delta, all 8) against koyo.hex20.space's public "Battery & Interface Card Temps" table, then linear-regression-fitting each candidate byte's raw value against the ground-truth temperature. All 4 channels fit at R²>0.998 with sub-0.1°C max residual - see CLAUDE.md §3/§4 for the full method and coefficients.
